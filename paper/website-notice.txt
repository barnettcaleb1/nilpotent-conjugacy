Cubic lower bounds for conjugacy separation in nilpotent groups
Caleb Barnett
Unverified draft — 6 October 2026

This draft proposes a family of torsion-free finitely generated nilpotent groups with conjugacy separability growth that would rule out a universal quadratic bound on the exponent in terms of Hirsch length. If verified, the construction, together with an existing upper bound, would establish cubic order for a universal exponent bound, up to constants.

Prepared with AI assistance. The arguments and claimed results have not yet been independently verified and should be treated as provisional. Comments and corrections are welcome.

The companion Lean package formalizes the explicit lower-bound construction, including the group invariants, finite separating witnesses, and the lower bound in every finite word metric. Its correspondence with the manuscript remains subject to independent review. The cited general conjugacy-separability and upper-bound theorems are outside the formalization. Code, checks, and exact scope: https://github.com/barnettcaleb1/nilpotent-conjugacy
